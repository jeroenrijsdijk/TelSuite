1 — Overview & two modes

Traffic Counter is a browser-based field tool for counting vehicles and pedestrians. All data is stored locally in the browser and exported to a semicolon-delimited CSV ready for Excel or KNIME. No account or internet connection required for counting — internet is only used for map tiles and address lookup.

Two modes are available:

Static mode Transect mode
Static mode STATIC

Stand at a fixed location on the roadside. One GPS fix is taken at the start. Counts are totalled per vehicle type and direction, with hourly extrapolation in the CSV.

Transect mode TRANSECT

Walk a route (dune path, shopping street, cycle path). GPS runs continuously throughout the session. Every count tap is tagged with the coordinates at that moment. A separate route log records your position every 10 seconds. A live map shows your route and count locations.

2 — Starting and ending a session

START button

Begins the session clock and activates the counting buttons. In Static mode, starts acquiring a one-time GPS fix. In Transect mode, starts the continuous GPS watch and route logging.

END button

Stops the clock, locks the counting buttons, and automatically downloads the CSV file. In Transect mode, captures one final GPS point and fits the map to the full route.

The status bar shows the current state at all times:

Press Start to begin counting
Counting in progress...
Session ended — downloading CSV...

The Export CSV button in the footer lets you download the data at any point during a session without ending it.

3 — Counting vehicles

Five vehicle types are available, top to bottom: Car, Truck/Van, Motorcycle, Bicycle, Pedestrian. Each row shows two +1 buttons — one per direction — and a small - button on the right side for quick per-row corrections.

The phone vibrates briefly on every tap as tactile confirmation, so you can count without looking at the screen.

Undo Last button

Removes the most recent count tap regardless of vehicle type or direction. The corresponding log entry is struck through. In Transect mode, the map marker for that tap is also removed. The button is greyed out until at least one count has been made.

4 — Directions & compass

The two direction labels (default: West / East) are free-text fields. Type anything that describes your situation — North, Inbound, Towards Station. These labels appear in the CSV columns.

Compass button

Point your phone along the road in one direction of travel, then tap Compass. The app reads the device compass, averages 5 readings for stability, and fills in the two opposing cardinal directions automatically (e.g. SW / NE). The button turns green when directions are set. On iOS 13+ you will be asked for motion sensor permission.

5 — GPS & location

Static mode STATIC

When you press Start, the app watches for GPS fixes and waits until the accuracy is 25 metres or better before locking in the position. It then calls the OpenStreetMap Nominatim API to look up the street name and city. The session panel shows live progress:

Waiting for GPS fix...Searching, no fix yet
GPS: +/-48m, improving...Fix found but still refining
Kerkstraat, AmsterdamReliable fix, address resolved
52.373, 4.892 (+/-18m)Reliable fix, no internet for address
... (low accuracy)Timed out after 30s, best fix used

The GPS fix, accuracy, street, city, country, and a reliability flag are all written to the CSV. The fix is marked reliable if accuracy was 25m or better.

Transect mode TRANSECT

GPS runs continuously for the entire session. The session panel shows the current accuracy in real time. Every +1 tap records the coordinates at that exact moment. A separate route point is saved every 10 seconds regardless of counting activity. Both logs appear in the CSV.

6 — Transect mode in detail

Transect mode is designed for counts taken while walking — dune paths, shopping streets, long cycle routes. The counting interface is identical to Static mode, but the following additional features are active:

Continuous GPS tracking

The GPS watch runs from Start to End. Each +1 tap stores the current coordinates. If GPS is temporarily lost (e.g. between dunes), the tap is still recorded with a note that GPS was unavailable at that moment.

Route log

Independent of counting, one GPS point is saved every 10 seconds. This captures your full walking route even in sections where nothing was counted. The number of route points is shown live in the session panel.

Live map

A Leaflet/OpenStreetMap map appears below the counters once the first route point is logged. A blue line traces your route, and coloured dots mark each count location (colour = vehicle type). Green dot = start, red dot = end. The map follows your position as you walk and zooms to fit the full route when you press End. Requires an internet connection for map tiles.

CSV structure

The transect CSV has three sections: a summary table (same as Static), a count log with timestamp and coordinates per tap, and the full route log. This lets you map every individual count in KNIME or GIS software.

7 — Location / Notes field

A free-text input below the status bar, always editable. Use it to describe the measurement location, weather conditions, nearby roadworks, or your name. The content is saved as the first data row in every CSV export.

8 — CSV export

All CSV files use semicolons as column delimiters and start with sep=; so Excel opens them correctly without the import wizard. All timestamps are in your local device timezone.

Static CSV structure

Header blockExport time, notes, start/end times, duration, GPS data (lat, lon, accuracy, street, city, country, reliability), extrapolation factor
Summary tableCounts per vehicle type and direction, plus per-hour extrapolations if session was 30s or longer
Timestamp logEvery individual tap: time, vehicle type, direction

Transect CSV structure

Header blockExport time, notes, start/end times, duration, route point count, extrapolation factor
Summary tableSame as Static
Count logEvery tap: timestamp, vehicle type, direction, latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy at that moment
Route logOne GPS point every 10 seconds: timestamp, latitude, longitude, accuracy
Hourly extrapolation

Calculated as count x (3600 / observed seconds). Requires a session of at least 30 seconds. The exact factor and observed duration are written to the CSV so you can verify or adjust the calculation in KNIME.

10 — Field tips

Before you start

Fill in the Notes field with the location name. Set direction labels, or use the Compass button while pointing along the road. In Static mode, wait for the GPS fix to reach a good accuracy before pressing Start.

Bright sunlight

The app uses a light background with high-contrast saturated colors for outdoor readability. Each vehicle type has a solid colored +1 button, so you can tap by color recognition without reading the label.

Correcting mistakes

Use the small - buttons on the right of each row to subtract from a specific vehicle/direction. Use Undo Last to remove the most recent tap of any type. In Transect mode the map marker is removed as well.

Long transect routes

For long routes, keep the screen on (turn off screen lock in your phone settings). The GPS and route logging run continuously even if you briefly pocket the phone, as long as the browser tab stays active. Battery usage is higher than normal due to continuous GPS — bring a powerbank for very long sessions.

Poor GPS coverage

In areas with poor signal (dense dune vegetation, narrow streets between tall buildings), GPS accuracy may be lower. Each count in the log records the accuracy at that moment, so you can filter out low-confidence points in KNIME later.

11 — Wat doen de parkeertellingen?

De parkeertellingen zijn veldtools voor wandelende parkeerobservaties. Je loopt een straat of route en registreert per parkeervak de toestand — links en rechts van de straat apart. De app snapped je positie op OSM-wegdata, plaatst markers loodrecht op de wegas en exporteert een ZIP met CSV, GPX en kaart-PNG.

Er zijn twee aparte apps: parkeertelling.html voor autoparkeren en fietsparkeren.html voor fietsparkeren. Beide werken identiek qua bediening.

13 — Sessie starten en stoppen

▶ Start

Activeert de GPS-watch. Telknoppen worden actief zodra de GPS-nauwkeurigheid 25 meter of beter is. Een oranje knipperende dot geeft aan dat de app nog op een goede fix wacht. Knoppen blijven 3 seconden actief bij een korte GPS-uitval (debounce).

■ Stop

Stopt de sessie. De ZIP-knop bovenaan de downloadrij wordt actief. Download-knoppen voor CSV, GPX en kaart blijven apart beschikbaar.

De Wake Lock — het slotje-icoontje in de header — houdt het scherm aan tijdens de sessie.

14 — Tellen: links en rechts

Het telscherm heeft twee kolommen gescheiden door een "WEG"-divider. Links = linkerkant van de straat in jouw looprichting, Rechts = rechterkant. Per kolom:

Knoplayout

Het eerste type loopt vol breed (meest gebruikt). De twee middelste types staan half naast elkaar. Het laatste type loopt weer vol breed onderaan.

Ongedaan maken

"↩ ongedaan maken" verwijdert de meest recente telling — ongeacht type of zijde. De marker op de kaart verdwijnt mee.

15 — Mini-kaart & bearing

Rechts in het straatpaneel staat een rond mini-kaartje van 90×90 px. Het toont de wegen binnen 100 meter, geroteerd zodat jouw looprichting altijd omhoog wijst. Bezochte wegvakken kleuren zandgeel.

De driehoekige pijl in het midden geeft de betrouwbaarheid van de bearing aan:

▲ GroenBewegend + kompas actief — meest betrouwbaar
▲ GeelgroenBewegend, alleen GPS
▲ GeelStilstaand, kompas dominant
▲ OranjeStilstaand, stale GPS-history
▲ RoodGeen bearing beschikbaar — tel nog niet
Kompasknop

Op iOS vraagt de "kompas"-knop toestemming voor DeviceOrientation. Eenmaal actief fust de app GPS-bearing (60%) en kompas (40%) bij beweging, en kompas-dominant (80%) bij stilstand. Kalibreer het kompas met een acht-beweging als de pijl structureel afwijkt.

16 — GPS & OSM wegsnapping

De gekleurde stip in de statusbalk toont de GPS-status: grijs = inactief, oranje knipperend = fix onvoldoende (>25m), groen = actief.

Wegvakken worden opgehaald via Overpass API (straal 300m). Markers komen 4 meter loodrecht op de dichtstbijzijnde wegas. Een indicator onder de straatnaam toont de snapstatus.

De OSM-wegrichting kan 180° verkeerd zijn t.o.v. looprichting. De app corrigeert dit automatisch door beide mogelijke richtingen te vergelijken met GPS+kompas en de best passende te kiezen.

Bij onbereikbaarheid van Overpass wordt automatisch een tweede eindpunt geprobeerd. Als beide falen, worden markers op de GPS-positie geplaatst.

17 — Crash recovery

Automatisch opslaan

De app slaat de sessie op in localStorage bij elke telling. Bij een crash of per ongeluk gesloten tab gaat niets verloren.

Bij heropenen verschijnt een herstelmelding als er een niet-afgesloten sessie is. Download-knoppen worden direct actief zodat de data exporteerbaar is.

18 — Exporteren (ZIP / CSV / GPX / PNG)

↓ Download ZIP

Bundelt CSV, GPX en kaart-PNG in één zipbestand. Op iOS/Android opent het deelmenu (AirDrop, Drive, WhatsApp, etc.). Op desktop gewone download. Dit is de aanbevolen manier om resultaten naar de computer te krijgen.

19 — Veldtips

Wacht op de groene pijl

Tel pas als de pijl in de mini-kaart groen of geelgroen is. Rood of oranje betekent dat de bearing onbetrouwbaar is en markers verkeerd geplaatst kunnen worden.

Looprichting

Houd de telefoon recht vooruit gericht met de bovenkant naar voren. Loop de route in één richting — bij een U-vormige route is een nieuwe sessie voor de terugweg nauwkeuriger.

Accu

Doorlopend GPS-gebruik vreet accu. Neem een powerbank mee bij sessies langer dan 45 minuten.
